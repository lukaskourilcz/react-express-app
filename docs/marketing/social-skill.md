## Carousel

Five slides, 1080 × 1350 px each, one question from the devShark question bank with its answer.

| Slide | Background | Text | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ink `#132019` | White | The question |
| 2 | Pale `#F3F6F1` | Ink | Context or the code |
| 3 | Green `#2D7A2D` | White | The options, or the moment before the answer |
| 4 | Pale `#F3F6F1` | Ink | The answer and why |
| 5 | Ink `#132019` | White | The takeaway, the full logo and the one mention of devShark |

- Put the clean fin, 48 px wide, in the same corner of every slide: white on ink and green, green on pale. Keep 48 px of margin between it and the slide edges.
- Show the full logo on slide 5 only. Use the horizontal logo at 160 px wide or more.
- Name devShark once, on slide 5. Slides 1 to 4 teach; they don't sell.
- Set code in JetBrains Mono on a pale or ink panel. No fake terminals or screenshots of made-up UI.

## Square post

1080 × 1080 px. One fact, question or result, the clean fin in the corner at 48 px, and the compact or horizontal logo at the bottom when the post names devShark.

## Story

1080 × 1920 px. Keep text and marks inside the middle 1080 × 1420 px: the platform covers about 250 px at the top and at the bottom with its own controls. The clean fin sits in the top corner of that safe area at 48 px. When the story links to devshark.app, the horizontal logo sits above the bottom safe line.

## QA checklist

Check every draft before it enters the approval queue:

- [ ] Every mark comes from `client/public/brand/v9/` at its original proportions.
- [ ] The fin tip points left, and the logo keeps the fin left of the wordmark.
- [ ] The clean fin sits at 48 px in the same corner of every slide.
- [ ] The full logo appears on the last slide only, with clear space the height of its letter d.
- [ ] The name reads devShark once, spelled exactly that way, never DevShark or DEVSHARK.
- [ ] Slide colours follow Ink, Pale, Green, Pale, Ink.
- [ ] Text contrast reaches 4.5:1: white on ink or green, ink on pale. Green text never sits on ink.
- [ ] The answer matches the question bank, and the draft makes no AI claim, price promise or feature the product lacks.
- [ ] Dimensions: 1080 × 1350 (carousel), 1080 × 1080 (square) or 1080 × 1920 (story).
- [ ] No gradients, shadows, 3D or generated imagery on the marks.
