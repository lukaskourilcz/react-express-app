# Higgsfield art direction

## Prompt foundation

> Editorial oceanic field journal for modern learning. Deep blue-green ink, off-white tactile paper, subtle print grain, scientific and cartographic linework, strong negative space, clean subject accents, restrained depth, natural imperfection, no generated text, no glossy 3D, no neon, no generic technology imagery.

## Composition

Use asymmetrical editorial balance, cropped layered paper, one clear focal mark, quiet background depth, and a text-safe region defined before generation. Prefer a flat plate or slight top-down field-journal perspective.

Avoid central floating objects on gradients, symmetric stock-poster composition, crowded collage, random UI cards, holograms, dramatic wide angles, lens flare, rim light, particles, and logo explosions.

## Product modifiers

- StudyShark: six restrained subject fragments held together by one ocean-ink/waterline structure.
- devShark: modular architecture flow, grid rhythm, deep green accent, no readable code.
- Subject plates: use the brand-system table; atmospheric/editorial only, never factual instruction.

## Negative prompt foundation

> text, letters, numbers, equations, code, logos, watermarks, interface screenshots, people, teams, students, robots, neon, cyberpunk, glassmorphism, glossy 3D, casino, money, flags, political borders, medical labels, gore, historical document, photorealistic evidence, impossible chess pieces, playing-card faces, clutter, lens flare, particles

## Production brief template

1. Asset ID and placement.
2. User/interface problem solved.
3. Required aspect ratios and intrinsic dimensions.
4. Text-safe and crop-safe regions.
5. Light/dark and mobile behavior.
6. Three genuinely different directions.
7. Rejection criteria and comparison notes.
8. Selected refinement and responsive variants.
9. Optimization, loading, alt classification and static fallback.
10. Manifest/provenance update and in-context QA.

## Motion language

If justified: slow current, subtle paper parallax, gentle wave, or a fin crossing a waterline. Never use rapid camera movement, zoom tunnels, energetic gaming effects or a loop that competes with reading. Always provide a poster and freeze under reduced motion.
