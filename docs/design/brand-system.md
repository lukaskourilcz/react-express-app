# Shark family brand system

## Architecture

```text
devShark — standalone developer-learning sibling
StudyShark — general-learning umbrella
  geoShark · mathShark · historyShark · bioShark · chessShark · pokerShark
```

The public names are exact. `webdev` is never a StudyShark picker subject. General brands are internal StudyShark contexts, not deployments. Product identity and links come from the existing registries.

## Shared identity

- Mark: a fin whose base meets a waterline. Use the existing deterministic SVG primitives and favicon family.
- Neutrals: ocean ink, off-white paper, muted blue-green surfaces.
- Surface: restrained grain, hairline edge, tactile bottom edge, quiet shadow.
- Typography: Manrope for editorial headings, Inter for reading/UI/code metadata with system fallbacks.
- Accent: one active product/subject accent plus semantic feedback colors.
- Image language: flat editorial plates, measured linework, authentic interface/data.

## Product and subject expression

| Brand | Content and mark language | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| devShark | Modular code structure, architecture flow, authentic technology glyphs, restrained grid rhythm. | Fake terminals/code, neon, cyberpunk, robots, bootcamp funnel. |
| geoShark | Contours, coordinates, atlas grids, terrain/current layers. | Fake borders/maps, random flags, geopolitical claims as decoration. |
| mathShark | Graph paper, construction lines, curves, coordinate fields, measured rhythm. | Generated equations or nonsense formulas. |
| historyShark | Archival layers, timeline rhythm, printmaking, artifact silhouettes. | Fake documents/handwriting, inaccurate documentary scenes, war glamour. |
| bioShark | Scientific linework, cell/system patterns, specimen-plate structure. | Gore, fake labels, uncanny anatomy, medical instruction from generation. |
| chessShark | Board geometry, move paths, tension, notation rhythm, negative space. | Impossible instructional positions, fantasy/luxury clichés. |
| pokerShark | Probability, table position, decision trees, restrained red. | Casino glamour, money, nightlife, readable generated cards, get-rich framing. |

## Voice

Concise, direct, active, specific, encouraging, honest, technically accurate, and natural in English and Czech. Prefer real actions: practise a topic, complete a level, review weak areas, join a classroom, report an answer. Avoid generic “unlock,” “revolutionize,” “supercharge,” “AI-powered,” and “future of education” claims.

## Free-forever rule

Support is voluntary and never affects content, explanations, paths, challenges, multiplayer, cards, scoring, XP, streaks, ranks, leaderboards, matching, accounts, or AI access. Cosmetics remain fairness-neutral. Support stays disabled until externally configured and owner-approved.
