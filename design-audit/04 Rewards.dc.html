Verdict

Needs work. This is the page most likely to make a developer think "startup toy": eight sections, a test-mode banner, a merchandise grid whose every tile says it cannot be redeemed yet, three "See what Premium includes" buttons, a coin bribe for following on Instagram, joke copy on a mug, and the same fairness disclaimer three times. The server-side design is careful and honest; the page shows all of that care as text. Cut it to what a free account can do today: see the balance and ledger, learn how coins are earned, invite a friend, buy the crown or a protection.

UX, states, mobile

Hierarchy. The balance is first, correctly. The second thing a free account can act on is the crown/protection, which is section six. With merchandise hidden (finding 2) and social removed (7), it becomes section four; move it above "Invite a friend".

Locked (Premium) state. A focusable "Redeem" with aria-disabled and a "Premium" word beside it is the right pattern. It only makes sense once redemption is open; at launch it is a locked door to an empty room.

Signed-out state. "Sign in to see your balance" plus the full earn list and merchandise grid: a visitor sees a shop before a product. The page should be signed-in only, or reduced to the balance card with the sign-in line.

Mobile. Eight sections × ~400px each is a 3,000px page. After the cuts it is roughly half. Rewards.css not read; merch tiles at minmax(240px) fit one per row at 360px.
