Verdict

Needs work. The structure is right (real sample question, real level names, an honest plan table) and the copy is mostly plain. What holds it back is repetition: the price is stated four times, three buttons scroll to the same anchor, "checkpoint" is explained three times, and the bottom third (founder note + pledge) restates the table. Cut those and the page gets shorter and more credible without a redesign.

UX, states, mobile

Hierarchy. The primary action is a scroll ("Choose a topic"); the real conversion is "Start JavaScript — Level 1" two screens down. Acceptable for an editorial landing, but only if the two later "Start free" buttons go: three CTAs to one anchor read as padding.

Mobile (≤760px, source: .ss-hero-grid, landingSections.css). Hero stacks; four stat tiles wrap into two rows of uppercase labels above the sample card, pushing the sample question below the fold. Removing two tiles (finding 3) fixes it. The plan table keeps three columns at 320px with 0.8rem text and 8px cell padding: dense but legible. Drawer lists 11 destinations for a signed-out visitor (finding 1).

Dark theme. Tokens are light-dark() pairs, so the page holds up. Two risks: the strip's four icon hues (warning/info/success/accent) sit at 8% tint on #0b141b and nearly vanish; the 4% watermark fin is invisible on ink and only adds paint. Not verified in a browser.

Accessibility. Strip items are <button>s with background:none;border:none and no focus style in the file: keyboard focus is the browser default, which the app otherwise overrides. Give them the .ss-fin-button ring or make them links. Stat tiles animate on hover (translateY + fin) but are not interactive (cursor:default) — a hover affordance on a non-control.
