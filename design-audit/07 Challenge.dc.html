Verdict

Needs work. The mechanics are honest and well engineered (server-graded timeouts, relaxed pace that is never ranked, a rewards replay when offline). The presentation is the arcade: a tagline headline in three fragments, "Enter the challenge", "Game over", "Hall of Fame", "Current champion", "Strike!", "Correct!", and a gold pill that promises the server is honest. The shark-fin lives are identity and stay; the arcade vocabulary goes.
