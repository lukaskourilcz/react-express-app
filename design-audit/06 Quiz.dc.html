In-quiz screen, states, mobile

In-quiz (not recreated). Header and footer hide; one 600px card, 82% max height, counter + waterline progress, category tag, optional #tag badges, question, radio-card answers, Previous/Next/Submit. The hashtag badges (finding 8) are the only noise. The soft tone on each pick (settings.soundEffects) should default off — check the default in lib/settings.ts (not read).

States. Loading (QuoteLoader "Preparing your questions."), error with Retry + Back to settings, empty-set guard, restore-in-progress from sessionStorage, leave-confirm dialog, receipt replay when a stats write fails. Complete and honest ("quiz.streakWarning" when a result could not be recorded).

Mobile. Pills wrap at 44px height; sixteen category pills plus "Show all" is two to three rows at 360px — acceptable for a setup screen that remembers itself. The result buttons wrap to two rows; with three buttons (finding 6) they fit one.

Dark theme. Category tags use devicon brand hex (JavaScript #f7df1e, etc.) with computed on-colour text in both themes — verified in lib/contrast.ts by design, not in a browser. The review explanation's coloured left border is the one place a brand colour is used as decoration on a neutral box.
