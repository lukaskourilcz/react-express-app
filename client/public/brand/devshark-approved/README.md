# devShark: the one-wave revision (superseded)

**Current kit: [`brand/v9`](../v9/README.md).** Ship nothing from this directory.

These assets came from the owner's Google Drive archive on 27 September 2026 and record the approved **one-wave + fin cut-out** revision that came before V9. `manifest.json` holds their provenance and SHA-256 hashes. The SVGs are vector artwork with outlined Manrope ExtraBold lettering; the PNG wordmarks have transparent backgrounds.

V9, chosen the same day, moves the fin up to the top ascender line, draws the wave as thick as the letter stems and sets the lettering optically bolder with rounded corners. The app, the favicon and the app icons use V9. [`docs/brand/brand-guidelines.md`](../../../../docs/brand/brand-guidelines.md) holds the current rules.

## Files

- Primary marks with the single wave: `logo-waves-{green,ink,white}.{svg,png}` (the plural in the name is historical)
- Secondary marks without the wave: `logo-clean-{green,ink,white}.{svg,png}`
- Fins: `fin-waves-*.svg` (with the wave) and `fin-clean-*.svg` (straight base)
- Favicons: `favicon.svg`, `favicon.ico` and `favicon-{16,32,48,180,192,512}.png`
