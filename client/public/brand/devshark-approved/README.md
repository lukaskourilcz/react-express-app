## Approved rules

- Exact wordmark: **devShark**. Lowercase d, e, v, h, a, r, k; uppercase S only. Bold Manrope ExtraBold is the starting point.
- The fin is left of the text. Its tip points left, away from the wordmark. Keep this silhouette and orientation across all variants.
- Primary logo: **one** wave line below the fin. The lower edge of the fin is cut out using the same wave rhythm. The cutout is transparent, not a painted background-colored patch.
- Favicon: the fin alone with a clean straight base, no wave line and no wave cutout. The existing clean version is also suitable for small embroidery.
- Green #2D7A2D, ink #132019, white #FFFFFF; supporting pale background #F3F6F1. Flat fills, no gradients or shadows in the master logo.
- `logo-clean-*` are secondary horizontal marks without waves; `logo-waves-*` are the primary marks with the approved single wave despite their historical plural filename.
- Do not revive the discarded text-inside-fin emblems, flattened fins, three-wave marks, or generative mockups with inconsistent silhouettes.
- Earlier discussions explored wave cuts in the lettering. The approved master has a solid, readable wordmark: do not assume lettering cutouts are mandatory.

## Brand-system development brief

Refine proportions, spacing, optical balance, wave integration, and small-size clarity without losing the approved identity. Develop horizontal and stacked lockups, symbol variants, monochrome/inverse versions, clear space, minimum sizes, color roles, type hierarchy, accessibility guidance, and practical export specifications. A stacked option should place the fin above the wordmark with optically aligned outer widths.

The product is devShark, a web-development learning application at https://devshark.app. Public product copy is English. It is not a generic AI product. Do not invent AI features, new subjects, pricing promises, or product claims.

Applications: web navigation, mobile, favicon/app icon, social avatar/cover, a restrained social template, and the existing merchandise categories: sticker set, mug, T-shirt, hoodie, cap. Initial layout proposals: 80 mm left-chest T-shirt logo, 90 mm hoodie logo, 85 mm mug logo, 35 mm clean-fin cap embroidery, 100 mm logo sticker. These are proposal dimensions, not supplier-certified production templates. Printing, cut lines, bleed, and embroidery digitization need the actual supplier template.
