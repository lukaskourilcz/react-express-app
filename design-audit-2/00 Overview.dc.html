Summary

1 · Leaderboard.

The structure round one praised is intact. What is out of step is the parts: the h1 is a claim and the page name is a kicker (no sibling does this); the period control fills the width where Profile uses underline tabs; the top three are filled ink discs, the one filled-ink shape in the product; the score column is Manrope at a literal 1.05rem; rows are 56px on a 16px gutter where Coins rows are 44px on 24px; the select, the “You” tag, the offline box and the visually-hidden utility are each a second copy of a shared rule; and a tab switch swaps the board for a six-row skeleton. Thirteen findings, all CSS or component swaps.

2 · Account.

Three breaks at the default 1000px width: the GitHub garden’s login, repository and date overflow their .ss-cost-grid cells (P0), “ROLE SPECIALIZATION” runs under the path title in its 5.5rem slot, and the paths card’s inventory uses the picker’s questions as labels, leaving the answers a ~100px ribbon. On phones the name and email are cut rather than ellipsised, the streak tiles squeeze the shield to ~100px between 396 and 520px, and a long rank title pushes the XP figure out of the career card and past the page edge. A five-digit stat crosses its tile at 1000px. The fixes are min-width: 0, grid minimums that count padding, one auto-fit and two short labels.

3 · Copy.

Ninety-one rows. The P1 set: three document titles and a page title still carrying pre-R1 names; the plan footnote saying “no ads” twice; five Learn end-states with the exclamation mark the P0.4 contract missed and “Not quite” for a wrong answer; the same career caveat three times on one page; “Level up”; “practice pace” as a second name for Relaxed pace; thirteen leftover competitor-table keys, two of which contradict the product; dead shop items one of which says “earns 2× XP”; the hero tagline; and random “dev tips” on the first-load screen. The rest is tightening.
